Contellab
Connecting Brands with Micro-Influencers and Content Creators
1. Project Name
Contellab
2. Project Idea
Contellab is a digital platform concept designed to connect brands and small businesses with micro-influencers and content creators.
The platform aims to make influencer marketing more accessible by helping brands discover suitable creators based on their niche, target audience, location, average views, and budget. It also helps creators discover collaboration opportunities with relevant brands.
The current project focuses on developing the marketing strategy and a prototype of the platform. AI-powered matching and content recommendations are potential future features and are not part of the current MVP.

4. Work Plan
The project follows the SOSTAC marketing planning framework:

Situation Analysis: Analyze the market, competitors, customers, and internal capabilities.
Objectives: Define SMART marketing objectives.
Strategy: Identify target segments, buyer personas, positioning, and the unique selling proposition (USP).
Tactics: Develop the marketing mix, content strategy, channels, and campaign approach.
Action: Create an implementation plan, assign responsibilities, and develop the project prototype.
Control: Define KPIs and methods to measure performance.

5. Roles & Responsibilities

Team Leader: Coordinate team activities, monitor progress, and ensure alignment with project requirements.
Market Research: Conduct competitor analysis and identify customer needs and market opportunities.
Marketing Strategy: Develop SMART objectives, buyer personas, positioning, and USP.
Content & Social Media: Plan content, messaging, and social media activities.
Implementation & Analysis: Support prototype development, campaign planning, KPI selection, and performance evaluation.

Note: Responsibilities will be finalized according to the actual distribution of tasks among team members.
6. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The following are proposed project KPIs and should be finalized according to the project's objectives and implementation scope:

Market Research: Complete competitor analysis and document key customer insights.
Target Audience: Develop and validate the primary buyer personas.
Marketing Strategy: Complete the SOSTAC marketing plan and define measurable SMART objectives.
Content & Social Media: Track reach, engagement rate, and relevant audience interactions when campaigns are implemented.
Prototype: Complete the planned prototype and test its main user flows.
Project Delivery: Complete and submit all required deliverables by the agreed deadline.
